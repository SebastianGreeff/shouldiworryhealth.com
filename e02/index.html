Do I Really Need a Knee Replacement?

Every factual claim in this video, when it is said, the study or guideline behind it, and how strong that evidence is. Watch the video on YouTube.

How to read this: a guideline or systematic review is the strongest evidence; an observational study or expert opinion is weaker, and the video says so where it matters. More on evidence levels.
