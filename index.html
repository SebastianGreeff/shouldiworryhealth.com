How we check the evidence

We use peer-reviewed research and published guidelines only; news articles, blogs and influencer content don't count as evidence. Each claim is labeled with the strongest kind of evidence behind it:

Guideline / systematic review
official guidance, or a study that pools every good study on the question
Randomized trial
people put into groups by chance, so differences are most likely due to the treatment
Observational study
researchers watched what happened without assigning treatment; weaker evidence
Expert opinion
textbook knowledge or clinical consensus with no single study behind it; weakest evidence

Where the evidence is weak or experts disagree, the video says so. If a claim turns out to be wrong, we correct it in a pinned comment, in the video description and on that episode's page here.
